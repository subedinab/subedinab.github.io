Multimodal LLMs · 3D vision · Agentic systems

I’m an M.S. student in Computer Science at the University of Wyoming, advised by Dr. Shivanand V. Sheshappanavar and Dr. Ahmed Abdelaty. My research connects vision, language, and 3D geometry to help AI reason about real engineering problems.

I’m fascinated by this question: How can we build AI systems that bring together multiple modalities to understand and interact with the real world?
