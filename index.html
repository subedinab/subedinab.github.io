Nabaraj Subedi’s Website

I am an M.S. student in Computer Science at the University of Wyoming, advised by Dr. Shivanand Venkanna Sheshappanavar (Geometric Intelligence Lab, EECS) and co-supervised by Dr. Ahmed Abdelaty (Civil & Architectural Engineering). My work is in multimodal retrieval-augmented generation, agentic AI, and 3D world models, with a focus on building grounded, compliance-aware systems for the built environment.

Research interests

  • Multimodal retrieval-augmented generation
  • Agentic AI and compliance reasoning
  • 3D world models and generative 3D
  • Vision–language models

Current and recent projects

ConstructGPT. A BIM-conditioned 3D latent diffusion and construction world model paired with a multimodal RAG compliance agent, trained on H100 GPUs. It reaches 0.806 VAE IoU on Rohbau3D and 0.534 leave-one-building-out world-model F1, and comes with a 417-sample BIM-anomaly benchmark. The goal is to automate construction-site monitoring and code-compliance checking directly from 3D scans against BIM. Accepted to the 2nd 3D-LLM/VLA Workshop at CVPR 2026.

Visual-first retrieval for civil standard plans. I work on retrieval that reads engineering drawings as images rather than flattening them to text, so question answering and compliance checking stay grounded in what the plan actually shows. A related line of work studies why adding more documents can hurt RAG, and uses domain-scoped, model-agnostic retrieval to keep vector search from diluting the signal.

WYDOT knowledge-graph assistant. A multi-agent RAG system over a Neo4j knowledge graph (150K+ nodes, 330K+ relationships) indexing 1,656+ Wyoming Department of Transportation engineering documents, with hybrid dense and sparse retrieval and a serverless backend on Google Cloud Run.
